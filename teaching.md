## Winter semester 2026/27

In the first semester at the University of Porto, I teach the following curricular units.

### [Statistical Inference (M4061)](https://sigarra.up.pt/fcup/en/ucurr_geral.ficha_uc_view?pv_ocorrencia_id=584316) and [Statistical Inference A (M4169)](https://sigarra.up.pt/fcup/en/ucurr_geral.ficha_uc_view?pv_ocorrencia_id=583677)

With [Eliana Duarte](https://sigarra.up.pt/fcup/en/func_geral.formview?p_codigo=668545). The two curricular units M4061 and M4169 are taught together. We will cover the basics of statistical inference, such as statistical models, point and interval estimation, confidence intervals, parametric and nonparametric hypothesis testing and Bayesian inference. The course also includes practical applications using the R programming language.

Prior experience with R is not required. The course will use R for selected exercises, so please install it before the first computational session. 

You can **Install R** from the [official CRAN website](https://cran.r-project.org/) and use it from your system's terminal. 
If you prefer using an IDE, then a popular choice is [RStudio Desktop](https://posit.co/download/rstudio-desktop/). R must be installed before RStudio. Further information is available in the [official RStudio getting-started guide](https://docs.posit.co/ide/user/ide/get-started/). To check your installation, open R or RStudio and run:

```r
R.version.string
mean(c(1, 2, 3))
```

If the second command returns 2, then your installation is working.
